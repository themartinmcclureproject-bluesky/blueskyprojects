Our Founders & Lived Experience

Forged from the toughest margins of human reality, built on unyielding accountability, and dedicated to systemic change across Cumbria.

Why We Built The Blue Sky Ecosystem

The Blue Sky Ecosystem Projects CIC was not born out of detached theory or corporate boardroom blueprints. It was forged in the fires of real survival, profound reckoning, and the absolute refusal to accept that people cannot change, heal, and rebuild.

We are Martin and Mira. Our story is complex, heavy, and deeply human. But more than anything, it is living proof that even after decades of trauma, systemic failure, and isolation, an individual and a family can claw their way back to purpose—and create something vital to protect others from walking that same lonely path.

Martin's Journey: Accountability, Crisis, and Redemption

Martin’s path represents a raw, uncompromising look at what happens when a human being is caught in the crushing gears of trauma, mental health crises, and the judicial system. As a survivor of childhood sexual abuse, Martin’s early life was fractured by deep trauma that manifested in dangerous ways—including substance use, severe mental health deterioration resulting in multiple psychiatric sections, involvement in crime, imprisonment, and the heavy, unescapable accountability of being an ex-perpetrator of domestic abuse.

Navigating the criminal justice and mental health systems over more than two decades is not something that can be neatly packaged. It involved hitting absolute rock bottom, facing one's own darkest actions, and undergoing the grueling, lifelong work of profound personal reconstruction and rehabilitation. Today, Martin stands as living evidence that redemption is real—that with rigorous self-accountability, stability, and structure, a person can completely transform their life, break generational cycles, and dedicate every ounce of their energy to stopping others from falling into those same abysses.

Mira’s Journey: Solitude, Bereavement, and Resilience

Mira’s foundation is rooted in a very different kind of trial—the crushing isolation of sudden bereavement while raising two children entirely alone. With her entire family located in another country and zero local support network to lean on, Mira faced the terrifying reality of grief compounded by total solitude. She did not know which way to turn, having to navigate the bureaucratic maze, emotional shockwaves, and daily survival mechanics with no map.

That experience of profound loneliness forged an unshakeable resilience and a fierce commitment to ensuring no other parent or individual in Cumbria ever has to face crisis without a hand to hold.

"We do not hide our pasts because our pasts are the exact warning lights and compass points for the future. We know what discrimination feels like. We know what systemic gaps look like. And we are here to bridge them."

Our Mission: Serious Action for Cumbria and Beyond

Our combined journeys give us a rare, unshakeable authority on human suffering, accountability, and recovery. We know how the justice system fails people, how mental health services stretch to breaking point, how domestic abuse damages families from all sides, and how grief can isolate a person in plain sight.

The Blue Sky Ecosystem Projects CIC exists because there is so much more work to do. We are here to dismantle stigma, eradicate discrimination against those trying to rebuild their lives, and construct practical, secure, non-judgmental micro-funding, educational, and emotional support networks—starting right here in Cumbria.

We lived through the storm so we could build the shelter. You don't have to walk it alone anymore.
