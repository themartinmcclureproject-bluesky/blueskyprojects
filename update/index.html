Our Vision: Local Roots, Global Reach

Something transformative is being built from the ground up.

The Blue Sky Ecosystems Projects CIC is a modern community ecosystem designed to uplift lives, strengthen grassroots charities, and foster human connection. We start locally in West Cumbria, building practical, lived-experience solutions to support frontline causes, families, and individuals navigating life's challenges.

But our horizon does not end at local borders. Today, the world sits in the palm of our hands:

  • 8.3 Billion People on Earth.
  • 6.13 Billion People connected online.
  • 5.75 Billion People holding a mobile device.

By leveraging technology, we aim to deliver meaningful support to anyone, anywhere—reaching people both digitally through mobile access and, in time, in person through expanding community networks.

What We Stand For

Lived Experience at the Core

Our initiative is grounded in real-life understanding, authentic empathy, and actionable growth. We bridge the gap between needing help and finding true, dignified support.

Grassroots Capability & Community Partnership

We stand alongside local charities, community hubs, and frontline organizations—providing shared capability and equipment to amplify the incredible work already happening on the ground.

Shared Stewardship

Our model ensures that resources remain accessible without financial barriers, while enabling residual goodwill and donor contributions to flow directly back into vital community causes.

Partner With Us

We are building out our digital infrastructure and establishing early community partnerships. Whether you are a local charity, community leader, donor, or global partner interested in supporting a lived-experience ecosystem, we welcome you to connect with us.
