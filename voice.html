Your Voice Matters

We are building this ecosystem for the community, with the community. Your lived experience, ideas, and perspectives are the foundation of everything we do.

Help Us Shape the Future

The Blue Sky Ecosystem Projects CIC was born directly from real lived experience. We know that the most powerful, enduring solutions come from those who have navigated the darkest times and emerged on the other side.

While we are currently in our foundational build stage—constructing our core workbooks, governance, and safeguarding frameworks—we are always listening. We believe that true support is not dictated from the top down, but built from the ground up.

Whether you have an idea for a community initiative, a perspective on how we can better support people, or you simply want to connect as we lay the groundwork for our future Men's and Women's Haven frameworks, we welcome your voice.
