Community Workbooks & Awareness Guides

Transforming every facet of our lived experience into actionable, structured support for those navigating their darkest chapters.

Currently in the Active Build Stage

Our educational ecosystem is vast. We are actively designing frameworks that cover the full spectrum of our survival—from navigating the justice system and institutionalization, to surviving domestic and sexual abuse, battling addiction, regulating impulses, and managing severe bereavement. These materials will not be released until they meet our rigorous standards for safety and professional review.

Our Future Resources

Free Awareness Guides

Knowledge is the first step toward resilience. We are developing a series of completely free, easily accessible awareness guides to provide immediate baseline support, crucial signposting, and clarity for individuals in distress and their families.

Structured Workbooks & Companion Guides

Drawing on decades of survival, we are writing deep-dive workbooks tailored to specific traumas and challenges. These companion guides offer non-clinical, empathetic, and actionable frameworks to help individuals rebuild their lives step by step.

Future Facilitated Courses

Our workbooks will eventually form the curriculum for our facilitated group sessions. These safe, structured environments will allow individuals to work through our materials collectively, fostering peer support and breaking the cycle of isolation.

Call for Volunteers: Safeguarding is Paramount

We are looking for passionate individuals with lived experience to help us complete our workbooks and prepare for our full launch. As we look toward the future of running our facilitated courses, our commitment to safety is absolute and non-negotiable.

To protect the vulnerable communities we serve, anyone seeking to become a volunteer facilitator must strictly complete:

  • Comprehensive Safeguarding Training
  • The appropriate AET (Award in Education and Training) Qualifications
  • A mandatory Enhanced DBS Check

These protocols are paramount to everything we create at The Blue Sky Ecosystem Projects.

Help Us Build the Foundation

If you have lived experience or professional expertise and want to volunteer your time to help us complete these workbooks, or if you wish to register your interest for future courses, we want to hear from you.
